BeaOps Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

BeaOps is an internal desktop tool made by Bea Studios to post Beeconomy videos to our own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Login tokens that TikTok, YouTube and Instagram issue when an account is connected.
  • Basic profile info from TikTok (display name and open ID), used only to show which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions you choose to post.

How it is stored

Everything stays on the computer running BeaOps. Login tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager. A local log of past posts (file name, title, result links) is kept on the same computer. Nothing is sent to Bea Studios servers or to anyone else.

How it is used

Data is used only to upload the videos you choose to the accounts you connected. We do not sell, share or analyse it, and we do not read anyone else's TikTok data.

Removing your data

Press Disconnect in BeaOps to delete the stored tokens, or remove BeaOps from your TikTok app settings under Security and permissions. Deleting the BeaOps folder removes the local post log.
